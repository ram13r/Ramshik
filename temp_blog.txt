Kundan jewellery is one of India's most treasured art forms, originating from the royal courts of Rajasthan and Gujarat. When paired correctly with the right saree, Kundan jewellery can transform your look from beautiful to absolutely breathtaking.

## What is Kundan Jewellery?

Kundan is a traditional form of Indian jewellery that involves setting highly refined gold foil (called Kundan) between gemstones and a gold framework. The result is a rich, intricate piece that looks magnificent in both natural and artificial light. Unlike regular gold jewellery, Kundan has an unpolished, natural finish that gives it an antique, royal appearance.

## Perfect Saree Pairings for Kundan Jewellery

### 1. Red Banarasi Silk + Classic Kundan Necklace Set
The most iconic combination in Indian bridal fashion. A deep red Banarasi silk saree with a full Kundan necklace set - maang tikka, earrings, and nath (nose ring) - is the gold standard of Indian bridal styling. The richness of the silk complements the opulence of the Kundan perfectly.

### 2. Royal Blue Saree + Gold Kundan Choker
A royal blue silk saree with a Kundan choker featuring ruby accents creates a look fit for royalty. Keep the earrings as statement drops and skip the necklace layers for a modern, refined look.

### 3. Pastel Pink Chiffon + Delicate Kundan Drops
For lighter colored sarees, choose Kundan pieces with pearl detailing. A thin Kundan necklace with matching jhumkas adds just the right amount of sparkle without overwhelming the softness of the saree.

### 4. Green Kanjeevaram + Emerald Kundan Set
Green silk sarees and Kundan set with emerald stones create a rich, co-ordinated look. This pairing is especially popular at South Indian weddings and receptions.

### 5. White or Cream Saree + Bold Kundan Maang Tikka
For contemporary brides who love minimalism, a simple white or cream saree with a bold, statement Kundan maang tikka and small Kundan jhumkas is an elegant modern choice.

## Styling Tips and Rules

**The Golden Rule: Let One Element Shine**
If your saree is heavily embellished (like a Banarasi or embroidered saree), choose simpler Kundan pieces. If your saree is plain or lightly patterned, you can go bold with your Kundan set.

**Layer Wisely**
Kundan necklace sets come in single, double and triple strands. For heavy sarees, stick to a single statement piece. For plain sarees at receptions, layering multiple necklace chains creates a regal look.

**Match Metals, Not Materials**
Kundan is always set in gold, so always pair it with gold-toned blouse buttons, pearl-embedded clutch bags, and gold bangles or kadas.

**Consider the Occasion**
- Wedding ceremony: Full Kundan bridal set (necklace, maang tikka, earrings, nath)
- Reception: Kundan choker + statement earrings
- Sangeet/Mehendi: Single statement Kundan earrings or a fun nath

## How to Care for Your Kundan Jewellery

Kundan jewellery requires gentle handling. Store each piece separately in a soft cloth pouch. Avoid contact with water, perfume, and harsh chemicals. Clean gently with a soft, dry cloth after use.
